How We Rank

A scoring system you can verify.

Every platform in the leaderboard goes through a four-stage editorial pass: a blind test-reading panel across love, career, money and destiny categories; a deep-dive on pricing, payout, and refund policies; aggregation of verified user reviews; and a once-a-month re-score.

We pay for every reading ourselves. No platform sees the scorecard until it is published. Advertisers cannot purchase coverage.

The editorial desk — Top Psychic Advisors

01

Blind test readings

Anonymous accounts, same questions, four categories. Scored on accuracy, specificity, and reader manner.

02

Pricing & policy audit

Per-minute rates, trial offers, refund policy, payout structure, hidden fees. Documented in a public sheet.

03

Verified-user aggregation

~470,000 reviews across Trustpilot, Sitejabber, app stores. Bot reviews and incentivized reviews are stripped.

04

Monthly recompute

Composite score = 0.4 test readings + 0.3 verified reviews + 0.2 value + 0.1 reader bench depth. Recalculated on the 1st of every month.
